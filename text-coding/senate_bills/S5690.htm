                                 A BILL


 
   To provide for the replacement of the plaque at Lincoln Memorial 
 commemorating the ``I Have a Dream'' speech of Martin Luther King, Jr.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Renewing the Dream Act''.

SEC. 2. REPLACEMENT OF PLAQUE AT LINCOLN MEMORIAL.

    (a) In General.--The Secretary of the Interior shall remove the 
plaque at Lincoln Memorial placed pursuant to Public Law 106-365 and 
install a new plaque commemorating the speech of Martin Luther King, 
Jr., known as the ``I Have a Dream'' speech.
    (b) Relation to CWA.--Chapter 89 of title 40, United States Code 
(commonly known as the ``Commemorative Works Act''), shall apply to the 
design and placement of the new plaque.
    (c) Acceptance of Contributions.--The Secretary of the Interior may 
accept contributions toward the cost of preparing and installing the 
new plaque without further appropriation.
    (d) Federal Funds.--Federal funds may be used to--
            (1) remove the plaque placed pursuant to Public Law 106-
        365; and
            (2) design, procure, and install the new plaque.
