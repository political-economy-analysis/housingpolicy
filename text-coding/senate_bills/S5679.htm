                                 A BILL


 
To prohibit the naming or renaming of a Naval vessel for an individual 
              serving as the President or Vice President.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``No Officeholder Self-Endorsement of 
Legacy for Navy Vessels Act'' or the ``NO SELF Act''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON NAMING OR RENAMING A NAVAL VESSEL FOR A SITTING 
              PRESIDENT OR VICE PRESIDENT.

    (a) In General.--The Secretary of the Navy may not name or rename a 
vessel of the Navy for an individual who, at the time of such naming or 
renaming, is serving as President or Vice President of the United 
States.
    (b) Permissible Naming or Renaming.--Nothing in this section shall 
prohibit the naming or renaming of a vessel for a former President or 
former Vice President after such individual leaves office.
