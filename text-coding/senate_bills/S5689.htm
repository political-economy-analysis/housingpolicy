                                 A BILL


 
    To prohibit Federal and State incentives for the development or 
                 commercial operations of data centers.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Recapturing Excessive Corporate 
Largesse through Accountability and Incentive Measures Act'' or the 
``RECLAIM Act''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON INCENTIVES FOR COVERED DATA CENTERS.

    (a) No Federal Incentives.--A covered data center (as defined in 
section 5000E(b) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as added by 
subsection (b)) shall not be eligible for any Federal grant, loan, loan 
guarantee, cooperative agreement, tax credit or deduction, exclusion, 
exemption, abatement, or other form of Federal financial assistance or 
tax incentive for the development or commercial operations of such 
covered data center.
    (b) Excise Tax on Prohibited State and Local Incentives.--
            (1) In general.--Subtitle D of the Internal Revenue Code of 
        1986 is amended by adding at the end the following new chapter:

 ``CHAPTER 50B--PROHIBITED STATE AND LOCAL INCENTIVES FOR DATA CENTERS

``Sec. 5000E. Prohibited state and local incentives for data centers.

``SEC. 5000E. PROHIBITED STATE AND LOCAL INCENTIVES FOR DATA CENTERS.

    ``(a) In General.--In the case of any covered data center, there is 
imposed a tax in an amount equal to 100 percent of the amount of any 
prohibited State or local incentive received by the data center 
operator during the taxable year with respect to such data center.
    ``(b) Definitions.--In this section--
            ``(1) Artificial intelligence.--The term `artificial 
        intelligence' has the meaning given such term in section 5002 
        of the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020 
        (15 U.S.C. 9401).
            ``(2) Covered data center.--
                    ``(A) In general.--Subject to subparagraph (B), the 
                term `covered data center' means any facility or 
                integrated campus of facilities--
                            ``(i) that has or is designed to have a 
                        contracted peak power demand of not less than 
                        100 megawatts, and
                            ``(ii) within which not less than 50 
                        percent of the installed compute accelerators 
                        are AI accelerator chips attributed to 
                        artificial intelligence training, fine-tuning, 
                        or inference.
                    ``(B) Regulations.--
                            ``(i) In general.--Not later than 1 year 
                        after the date of enactment of this section, 
                        the Secretary (in conjunction with the 
                        Secretary of Commerce) shall promulgate 
                        regulations that define the term `AI 
                        accelerator chip' for purposes of subparagraph 
                        (A)(ii).
                            ``(ii) Required considerations.--In 
                        promulgating the regulations required under 
                        clause (i), the Secretary (in conjunction with 
                        the Secretary of Commerce) shall consider 
                        whether the definition of the term `AI 
                        accelerator chip' should include graphics 
                        processing units, tensor processing units, 
                        application-specific integrated circuits, and 
                        other semiconductor devices designed primarily 
                        for artificial intelligence training or 
                        inference.
                            ``(iii) Adjustments.--The Secretary (in 
                        conjunction with the Secretary of Commerce) may 
                        promulgate regulations--
                                    ``(I) adjusting the thresholds 
                                described in clauses (i) and (ii) of 
                                subparagraph (A) for high-density 
                                facilities optimized for artificial 
                                intelligence, and
                                    ``(II) excluding de minimis 
                                computing sites sitting on the edge of 
                                those thresholds.
            ``(3) Data center operator.--The term `data center 
        operator' means the person that enjoys the economic benefits 
        from, and bears a majority of the economic burdens related to, 
        ownership of the covered data center.
            ``(4) Prohibited state or local incentive.--The term 
        `prohibited State or local incentive' means any State or local 
        grant, loan, loan guarantee, cooperative agreement, tax credit 
        or deduction, exclusion, exemption, abatement, or other form of 
        State or local financial assistance or tax incentive for the 
        development or commercial operations of a covered data center.
    ``(c) Regulations and Guidance.--Not later than the date that is 12 
months after the date of enactment of this section, the Secretary shall 
issue such regulations or other guidance as may be necessary or 
appropriate to carry out the purposes of this section, including any 
regulations or guidance for purposes of--
            ``(1) determining the value of any prohibited State or 
        local incentive,
            ``(2) coordinating the tax imposed under subsection (a) 
        with any other relevant Federal tax liability associated with a 
        covered data center, including rules to prevent double taxation 
        or double counting with respect to any prohibited State or 
        local incentive, and
            ``(3) the timing of recognition of a prohibited State or 
        local incentive as received, including rules addressing 
        incentives received in installments or contingent on future 
        performance.''.
            (2) Clerical amendment.--The table of chapters for subtitle 
        D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by inserting 
        after the item relating to chapter 50A the following new item:

     ``Chapter 50B--Prohibited State and Local Incentives for Data 
                               Centers''.

    (c) Sense of Congress.--It is the sense of Congress that States and 
political subdivisions thereof should promptly repeal or reform any tax 
incentive programs that are specific to data centers in order to 
restore tax neutrality, protect public revenues, and eliminate 
distortions in business location decisions that disadvantage domestic 
manufacturing and other job-creating investments.
    (d) Effective Dates.--The provisions of this section, and any 
amendment to the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 made by this section, 
shall take effect on the day after the date on which final regulations 
are issued by the Secretary pursuant to section 5000E(c) of the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (as added by subsection (b)).

SEC. 3. SEVERABILITY.

    If any provision of this Act or amendment made by this Act or the 
application of such provision or amendment to any person or 
circumstance is held to be unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act 
and the amendments made by this Act, and the application of the 
provision or amendment to any other person or circumstance, shall not 
be affected.
