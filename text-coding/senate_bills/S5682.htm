                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as amended to 
  clarify that foreign government-owned media operating in the United 
                States must register as a foreign agent.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Foreign Government-Owned Media FARA 
Registration Act''.

SEC. 2. DEFINITION.

    Section 1 of the Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as 
amended (22 U.S.C. 611) is amended by striking subsection (d) and 
inserting the following:
    ``(d) The term `agent of a foreign principal'--
            ``(1) includes any news or press service or association 
        operating in the United States that is owned, controlled, or 
        financed directly or indirectly, in whole or in part, by a 
        government of a foreign country, unless that news or press 
        service or association--
                    ``(A) has the freedom, pursuant to the law of that 
                foreign country, to take any position adverse to the 
                foreign government owning, controlling, or financing 
                the news or press service or association; and
                    ``(B) can demonstrate to the satisfaction of the 
                Attorney General that the news or press service or 
                association is independent in all matters relating to 
                the editorial decisions of the news or press service or 
                association; and
            ``(2) does not include any news or press service or 
        association organized under the laws of the United States or of 
        any State or other place subject to the jurisdiction of the 
        United States, or any newspaper, magazine, periodical, digital 
        media, or other publication for which there is on file with the 
        United States Postal Service information in compliance with 
        section 3685 of title 39, United States Code, published in the 
        United States, solely by virtue of any bona fide news or 
        journalistic activities, including the solicitation or 
        acceptance of advertisements, subscriptions, or other 
        compensation therefor, so long as it is at least 80 per centum 
        beneficially owned by, and its officers and directors, if any 
        are citizens of the United States, and such news or press 
        service or association, newspaper, magazine, periodical, 
        digital media, or other publication, is not owned, directed, 
        supervised, controlled, subsidized, or financed, and none of 
        its policies are determined by any foreign principal defined in 
        subsection (b) of this section, or by any agent of a foreign 
        principal required to register under this Act.''.

SEC. 3. REPORTS TO THE CONGRESS.

    The Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as amended (22 U.S.C. 
611 et seq.) is amended by striking section 11 (22 U.S.C. 621) and 
inserting the following:

                       ``reports to the congress

    ``Sec. 11.  (a) The Attorney General shall provide an annual report 
to the Congress concerning administration of this Act, including 
registrations filed pursuant to this Act, and the nature, sources and 
content of political propaganda disseminated and distributed.
    ``(b) In each report submitted under subsection (a), the Attorney 
General shall include in the report--
            ``(1) a list of each foreign media outlet operating in the 
        United States that is owned, controlled, or financed directly 
        or indirectly, in whole or in part, by a government of a 
        foreign country; and
            ``(2) with respect to each foreign media outlet described 
        in paragraph (1)--
                    ``(A) a statement of whether the foreign media 
                outlet is registered under this Act; and
                    ``(B) if the foreign media outlet is not registered 
                under this Act, an explanation of why the foreign media 
                outlet is not registered under this Act.''.
