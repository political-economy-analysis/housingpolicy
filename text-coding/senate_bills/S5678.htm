                                 A BILL


 
To require the Secretary of the Navy to establish a process for naming 
               of Naval vessels, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Navy Accountability and Military 
Eponym Standards Act'' or the ``NAMES Act''.

SEC. 2. ESTABLISHMENT OF PROCESS FOR NAMING OF VESSELS OF THE NAVY.

    (a) In General.--The Secretary of the Navy shall--
            (1) establish and publish objective criteria for naming and 
        renaming vessels of the Navy;
            (2) obtain a written historical assessment from the Naval 
        History and Heritage Command before naming or renaming a 
        vessel;
            (3) provide a written rationale for the selected name for 
        the vessel;
            (4) explain any departure from the primary naming 
        recommendation of the Naval History and Heritage Command with 
        respect to the vessel; and
            (5) when renaming a vessel, explain the historical 
        significance of the existing name and the reasons for removing 
        it.
    (b) Written Determination.--The Secretary shall provide any written 
determination required under subsection (a) to the congressional 
defense committees (as defined in section 101(a)(16) of title 10, 
United States Code) and make it publicly available, subject to 
applicable law.
