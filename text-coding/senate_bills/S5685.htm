                                 A BILL


 
 To require the disclosure of supervisory records of certain financial 
  institutions that relate to Jeffrey Epstein, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Epstein Wall Street Records Act of 
2026''.

SEC. 2. EPSTEIN SUPERVISORY RECORDS.

    (a) Definitions.--In this section:
            (1) Covered institution.--The term ``covered institution'' 
        means any current or former financial institution with known or 
        alleged ties to--
                    (A) Jeffrey Epstein;
                    (B) any entity owned or controlled by Jeffrey 
                Epstein, whether directly or through any representative 
                of Jeffrey Epstein;
                    (C) Ghislaine Maxwell; or
                    (D) an associate of Jeffrey Epstein or Ghislaine 
                Maxwell.
            (2) Covered record.--The term ``covered record'' means a 
        supervisory record (including an examination report, the notes 
        of an examiner, a supervisory communication between a financial 
        regulatory agency and a covered institution, an investigatory 
        filing, a matter requiring attention, a matter requiring 
        immediate attention, an internal communication of a financial 
        regulatory agency regarding a covered institution, and any 
        other record created or obtained in connection with the 
        financial regulatory agency's supervision of a covered 
        institution) that--
                    (A) a financial regulatory agency maintains with 
                respect to a covered institution; and
                    (B) relates to--
                            (i) Jeffrey Epstein;
                            (ii) any entity owned or controlled by 
                        Jeffrey Epstein, whether directly or through 
                        any representative of Jeffrey Epstein;
                            (iii) Ghislaine Maxwell; or
                            (iv) an associate of Jeffrey Epstein or 
                        Ghislaine Maxwell.
            (3) Financial regulatory agency.--The term ``financial 
        regulatory agency'' means--
                    (A) the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve 
                System;
                    (B) the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency;
                    (C) the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation; and
                    (D) the Securities and Exchange Commission.
            (4) Senior executive.--The term ``senior executive'', with 
        respect to a covered institution--
                    (A) means a current or former director, officer, 
                controlling stockholder, or partner of the covered 
                institution, or any other employee of the covered 
                institution with substantial managerial, compliance, 
                investment, or client relationship management 
                responsibilities; and
                    (B) does not include an employee of the covered 
                institution who was not involved in the management of 
                the covered institution.
    (b) Production of Supervisory Records.--Notwithstanding any other 
provision of law or regulation, each financial regulatory agency 
shall--
            (1) not later than 90 days after the date of enactment of 
        this Act, submit to the Chairman and Ranking Member of the 
        Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs of the Senate, 
        the Chairman and Ranking Member of the Committee on Financial 
        Services of the House of Representatives, the Chairman and 
        Ranking Member of the Committee on Homeland Security and 
        Governmental Affairs of the Senate, and the Chairman and 
        Ranking Member of the Committee on Oversight and Government 
        Reform of the House of Representatives physical copies of all 
        covered records maintained by the financial regulatory agency;
            (2) not later than 120 days after the date of enactment of 
        this Act, make publicly available in a searchable and 
        downloadable format all covered records maintained by the 
        financial regulatory agency, except that, in carrying out this 
        paragraph, a financial regulatory agency shall withhold or 
        redact the segregable portion of a covered record that contains 
        personally identifiable information of victims (or the personal 
        or medical files of victims); and
            (3) provide a written description of the basis for each 
        withholding and redaction under paragraph (2).
    (c) Investigations.--
            (1) In general.--Each financial regulatory agency shall, 
        immediately after the date of enactment of this Act, commence 
        investigations into covered institutions and senior executives 
        with respect to covered institutions.
            (2) Subjects of investigations.--Each investigation shall--
                    (A) determine whether a covered institution or 
                senior executive violated Federal banking, securities, 
                or anti-money laundering laws; and
                    (B) evaluate--
                            (i) the extent to which the covered 
                        institution, and senior executives with respect 
                        to the covered institution, conducted business 
                        with Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, or an 
                        associate of Jeffrey Epstein or Ghislaine 
                        Maxwell;
                            (ii) the extent to which the covered 
                        institution, and senior executives with respect 
                        to the covered institution, failed to 
                        reasonably oversee their employees who 
                        willfully or negligently facilitated efforts by 
                        Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, or an 
                        associate of Jeffrey Epstein or Ghislaine 
                        Maxwell to evade compliance, regulatory, and 
                        supervisory controls and scrutiny relating to 
                        the business and transactions of that covered 
                        institution;
                            (iii) the monetary value of the business 
                        relationship between the covered institution 
                        and Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, or an 
                        associate of Jeffrey Epstein or Ghislaine 
                        Maxwell;
                            (iv) the extent to which the covered 
                        institution had sufficient controls in place to 
                        identify illicit activity conducted by Jeffrey 
                        Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, or an associate of 
                        Jeffrey Epstein or Ghislaine Maxwell, whether 
                        those controls were ever exercised, and the 
                        outcome of exercising those controls;
                            (v) any communication between the covered 
                        institution and Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine 
                        Maxwell, or an associate of Jeffrey Epstein or 
                        Ghislaine Maxwell; and
                            (vi) whether any documents related to 
                        Jeffrey Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell, or an 
                        associate of Jeffrey Epstein or Ghislaine 
                        Maxwell were destroyed, and, if so, when and 
                        why the documents were destroyed and who 
                        authorized the destruction.
    (d) Mandatory Consideration of Industry Prohibition and Civil 
Penalties for Executive Misconduct.--
            (1) Federal reserve; fdic; occ.--
                    (A) In general.--The Board of Governors of the 
                Federal Reserve System, the Federal Deposit Insurance 
                Corporation, or Comptroller of the Currency shall 
                impose 1 or more of the remedies described in 
                subparagraph (B) if, in the course of conducting an 
                investigation required under subsection (c), the Board, 
                Corporation, or Comptroller, as applicable, determines, 
                following notice and an opportunity for hearing, that a 
                senior executive of an insured depository institution--
                            (i) violated or aided or abetted a 
                        violation of Federal banking laws or Federal 
                        anti-money laundering laws in connection with 
                        the financial accounts or transactions of 
                        Jeffrey Epstein; or
                            (ii) engaged in conduct that otherwise 
                        provides a basis for a sanction under 
                        subsection (e) or (i) of section 8 of the 
                        Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1818).
                    (B) Remedies.--The remedies described in this 
                subparagraph are the following:
                            (i) An order prohibiting any participation 
                        by that senior executive, in any manner, in the 
                        conduct of the affairs of any insured 
                        depository institution described in section 
                        8(e) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 
                        U.S.C. 1818(e)).
                            (ii) An order imposing a civil penalty 
                        described in section 8(i)(2)(C) of the Federal 
                        Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 
                        1818(i)(2)(C)).
            (2) SEC.--
                    (A) In general.--The Securities and Exchange 
                Commission shall seek or impose 1 or more of the 
                remedies described in subparagraph (B) if, in the 
                course of conducting an investigation required under 
                subsection (c), the Commission determines, following 
                notice and an opportunity for a hearing, that a senior 
                executive of a broker, dealer, investment adviser, 
                municipal securities dealer, municipal advisor, 
                transfer agent, or nationally recognized statistical 
                rating organization--
                            (i) violated or aided or abetted a 
                        violation of Federal securities laws or Federal 
                        anti-money laundering laws in connection with 
                        the financial accounts or transactions of 
                        Jeffrey Epstein; or
                            (ii) engaged in conduct that otherwise 
                        provides a basis for a sanction under section 
                        203(f) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 
                        (15 U.S.C. 80b-3(f)) or section 15(b)(6) of the 
                        Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 
                        78o(b)(6)).
                    (B) Remedies.--The remedies described in this 
                subparagraph are the following:
                            (i) An order barring the senior executive 
                        from association with any broker, dealer, 
                        investment adviser, municipal securities 
                        dealer, municipal advisor, transfer agent, or 
                        nationally recognized statistical rating 
                        organization pursuant to section 15(b)(6) of 
                        the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 
                        78o(b)(6)) or section 203(f) of the Investment 
                        Advisers Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. 80b-3(f)).
                            (ii) A civil penalty described in section 
                        203(i)(2)(C) of the Investment Advisers Act of 
                        1940 (15 U.S.C. 80b-3(i)(2)(C)) or section 
                        21B(b)(3) of the Securities Exchange Act of 
                        1934 (15 U.S.C. 78u-2(b)(3)) in a court of the 
                        United States.
    (e) Criminal Referrals.--If, in the course of conducting an 
investigation required under subsection (c), the financial regulatory 
agency reasonably finds that a violation of Federal criminal law may 
have occurred, that financial regulatory agency shall refer the matter 
to the Attorney General.
    (f) Report.--Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of 
this Act, the Vice Chairman for Supervision of the Board of Governors 
of the Federal Reserve System, the Comptroller of the Currency, the 
Chairperson of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, and the 
Chairman of the Securities and Exchange Commission shall submit to the 
Chairman and Ranking Member of the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
Urban Affairs of the Senate, the Chairman and Ranking Member of the 
Committee on Financial Services of the House of Representatives, the 
Chairman and Ranking Member of the Committee on Homeland Security and 
Governmental Affairs of the Senate, and the Chairman and Ranking Member 
of the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform of the House of 
Representatives a report detailing the findings and the outcomes of the 
investigations required under subsection (c) and any criminal referral 
made to the Attorney General.
