                                 A BILL


 
  To amend the Mineral Leasing Act for Acquired Lands and the Mineral 
Leasing Act to increase the State share of oil and gas leasing revenue 
              from acquired lands, and for other purposes.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Acquired Lands Revenue Modernization 
Act''.

SEC. 2. DISPOSITION OF RECEIPTS FROM OIL AND GAS LEASES OF ACQUIRED 
              LANDS.

    (a) Mineral Leasing Act for Acquired Lands.--Section 6(a) of the 
Mineral Leasing Act for Acquired Lands (30 U.S.C. 355(a)) is amended, 
in the first sentence--
            (1) by striking ``Subject to the provisions of section 
        35(b) of the Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 191(b)), all'' and 
        inserting ``All''; and
            (2) by striking ``for other receipts'' and all that follows 
        through ``such lands'' and inserting ``in section 35 of the 
        Mineral Leasing Act (30 U.S.C. 191)''.
    (b) Mineral Leasing Act.--Section 35(a) of the Mineral Leasing Act 
(30 U.S.C. 191(a)) is amended, in the first sentence--
            (1) by striking ``, and rentals'' and inserting ``(30 
        U.S.C. 1701 et seq.), rentals''; and
            (2) by inserting ``(30 U.S.C. 1001 et seq.), and oil and 
        gas leases under the Mineral Leasing Act for Acquired Lands (30 
        U.S.C. 351 et seq.)'' after ``Geothermal Steam Act of 1970''.
