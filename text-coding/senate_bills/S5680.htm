                                 A BILL


 
    To prohibit the use of Federal funds to rename, redesignate, or 
 otherwise change the name of the vessel designated as CVN-81 from the 
                         ``USS Doris Miller''.

    Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled,

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

    This Act may be cited as the ``Maintaining Integrity of Military 
Legacy and Enduring Recognition Act'' or the ``MILLER Act''.

SEC. 2. PROHIBITION ON USE OF FUNDS TO RENAME, REDESIGNATE, OR 
              OTHERWISE CHANGE THE NAME OF CVN-81 FROM USS DORIS 
              MILLER.

    No Federal funds may be obligated or expended to rename, 
redesignate, or otherwise change the name of the vessel designated as 
CVN-81 from the ``USS Doris Miller''.
